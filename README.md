## Entry

`main.py` writes one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> doomscrolling

The internet in one word: you came for one thing, stayed for everything, and left knowing less than when you arrived. Chosen because it is funny on its own, true of the whole internet rather than one app, and not the first obvious word anyone reaches for. Standard library only, no network, no third-party packages, deterministic.
